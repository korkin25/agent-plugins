# OrbitNotes 4.2.0 Release Notes

Released 2027-03-04. This update is free for all users of Orbit notes 4.x.

## New Features

- Shared notebooks now support up to 8 editors at the same time, and every editor sees changes in under 250 ms.
- You can attach files of up to 25 MB to a note; images larger than 2 MB are compressed automatically.
- The web site now offers 3 new themes and 12 new templates.
- E-mail digests now arrive weekly and list the 5 notes you edited most often.

## Fixes And Improvements

- Search results load 40% faster in notebooks with more than 2000 notes.
- Fixed a crash that happened when you logged in on 2 devices within 1 minute.
- Exports to PDF no longer drop the last 4 pages of long notes.
- Sync now retries 3 times before it shows an error, with a 500 ms pause between attempts.

## How To Log In With Your Company Account

To log in with your company account, open the web site, choose "Use company account" and enter your work e-mail address. If you are already logged in on another device, Orbit Notes keeps that session active for 7 days.

## Known Issues

- On some tablets the editor toolbar hides 1 of its 6 buttons. A fix is planned for version 4.2.1, scheduled for 2027-04-15.
- Users who logged in before 2027-03-04 may need to log in again once.

## Upgrading OrbitNotes

Version 4.2.0 needs 2 GB of free space and about 9 minutes to install.
