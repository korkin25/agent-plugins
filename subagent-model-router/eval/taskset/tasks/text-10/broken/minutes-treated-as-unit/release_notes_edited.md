# Orbit Notes 4.2.0 release notes

Released March 4, 2027. This update is free for all users of Orbit Notes 4.x.

## New features

- Shared notebooks now support up to eight editors at the same time, and every editor sees changes in under 250 ms.
- You can attach files of up to 25 MB to a note; images larger than 2 MB are compressed automatically.
- The website now offers three new themes and 12 new templates.
- Email digests now arrive weekly and list the five notes you edited most often.

## Fixes and improvements

- Search results load 40% faster in notebooks with more than 2000 notes.
- Fixed a crash that happened when you signed in on two devices within 1 minute.
- Exports to PDF no longer drop the last four pages of long notes.
- Sync now retries three times before it shows an error, with a 500 ms pause between attempts.

## How to sign in with your company account

To sign in with your company account, open the website, choose "Use company account" and enter your work email address. If you are already signed in on another device, Orbit Notes keeps that session active for seven days.

## Known issues

- On some tablets the editor toolbar hides one of its six buttons. A fix is planned for version 4.2.1, scheduled for April 15, 2027.
- Users who signed in before March 4, 2027 may need to sign in again once.

## Upgrading Orbit Notes

Version 4.2.0 needs 2 GB of free space and about 9 minutes to install.
