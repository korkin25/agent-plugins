On 2027-01-14 the public payment API, which merchants use to create charges, stopped accepting new connections and stayed down for 47 minutes, during which 1,284 payment requests failed. The root cause was an expired TLS certificate on load balancer lb-east-2: the automatic renewal job had been failing silently for weeks after a permissions clean-up removed its write access. Tomas Adeyemi issued and deployed a new certificate manually. No card data was exposed, and the Platform team will add alerts before certificates expire.
