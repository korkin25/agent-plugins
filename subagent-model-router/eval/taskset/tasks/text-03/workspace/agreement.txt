MAINTENANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made between:

(1) ORCHARD VALE LOGISTICS LIMITED, a company registered in England and Wales
    under company number 08812457, whose registered office is at Unit 4,
    Fenwick Trading Estate, Marlow Cross (the "Client"); and

(2) BRIGHTWATER SYSTEMS LTD, a company registered in England and Wales under
    company number 11290345, whose registered office is at 22 Tannery Lane,
    Ashcombe (the "Provider").

BACKGROUND

(A) The Client operates a parcel sorting centre at Marlow Cross that relies on
    conveyor and sorting equipment supplied by a third party.
(B) The Provider maintains conveyor and sorting equipment of that kind and has
    agreed to maintain the Client's equipment on the terms of this Agreement.

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement:
    "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public
    holiday in England;
    "Contract Year" means each period of twelve (12) months starting on the
    Effective Date or on an anniversary of it;
    "Effective Date" means 04/03/2027;
    "Monthly Fee" means the fee set out in clause 4.1;
    "Services" means the preventive and corrective maintenance of the
    equipment listed in Schedule 1.
1.2 All dates in this Agreement are written in the form day/month/year.
1.3 Clause headings do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial
    term of twenty-four (24) months (the "Initial Term").
2.2 After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive
    periods of twelve (12) months each (each a "Renewal Period") unless either
    party terminates it under clause 11.

3. SERVICES

3.1 The Provider shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care and
    in accordance with good industry practice.
3.2 The Provider shall respond to a critical fault within four (4) hours of
    the Client's report if the report is made on a Business Day, and within
    eight (8) hours in all other cases.
3.3 The Provider shall carry out one preventive maintenance visit every month
    and shall give the Client at least five (5) Business Days' notice of each
    visit.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Client shall pay the Provider a Monthly Fee of GBP 12,500 (twelve
    thousand five hundred pounds sterling), exclusive of VAT.
4.2 The Provider shall invoice the Monthly Fee in advance on the first Business
    Day of each month.
4.3 The Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of the
    date of the invoice.
4.4 Any sum not paid when due bears interest at the rate of 1.5% per month from
    the due date until the date of payment.
4.5 Call-out visits requested by the Client outside the Services are charged at
    GBP 95 per hour, in addition to the Monthly Fee.

5. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

5.1 The Client shall give the Provider's engineers safe access to the equipment
    during the Client's normal operating hours.
5.2 The Client shall not allow anyone other than the Provider to modify the
    equipment's control software during the term of this Agreement.

6. SPARE PARTS

6.1 Spare parts with a list price below GBP 250 per item are included in the
    Monthly Fee. Other spare parts are charged at list price less ten per cent
    (10%).

7. INSURANCE

7.1 The Provider shall maintain public liability insurance of at least
    GBP 5,000,000 for each claim throughout the term of this Agreement.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret
    during the term of this Agreement and for three (3) years after it ends.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits either party's liability for death or
    personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.
9.2 Subject to clause 9.1, each party's total liability under or in connection
    with this Agreement in any Contract Year is limited to an amount equal to
    twelve (12) times the Monthly Fee.

10. GUARANTEE

10.1 The Provider's parent company, Brightwater Systems Holdings plc,
     guarantees the Provider's performance of this Agreement under a separate
     deed of guarantee.

11. TERMINATION

11.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement at the end of the Initial Term
     or of any Renewal Period by giving the other party not less than ninety
     (90) days' written notice.
11.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written
     notice if the other party commits a material breach and does not remedy
     it within thirty (30) days of being asked in writing to do so.

12. NOTICES

12.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the
     registered office of the receiving party.

13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

13.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising from it are governed by the law of
     England and Wales.
13.2 The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

14. CHANGES AGREED BEFORE SIGNATURE

14.1 After the draft of this Agreement was circulated, the parties agreed the
     following changes. Each change prevails over the text of the clause it
     amends:
     (a) in clause 4.1, the Monthly Fee of GBP 12,500 is replaced by GBP 11,800
         (eleven thousand eight hundred pounds sterling);
     (b) in clause 11.1, "ninety (90) days'" is replaced by "sixty (60) days'".

SCHEDULE 1 - EQUIPMENT

Line A: inbound conveyor, 140 m, with two merge units.
Line B: cross-belt sorter with 220 carriers and 96 chutes.
Line C: outbound conveyor, 85 m, with one spiral lowerator.
